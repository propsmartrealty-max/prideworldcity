Why Pride World City Leads This Segment

Pune Airport — 10-Minute Doorstep Access

Via Lohegaon-Charholi Road and Nagar Road, Pune International Airport is reachable in under 10 minutes without signal delays in off-peak hours. This makes Pride World City the only 400-acre township in Pune within 10 minutes of an international airport.

Kharadi EON IT Park — 18 Minutes Peak Hour

Kharadi EON IT Park hosts 150+ IT companies including top MNCs. The 18-minute peak-hour commute via Nagar Road and Wagholi Road makes Pride World City the most logical residential base for Kharadi employees seeking township-grade living.

Pune Ring Road (PRR) — 1.8 km Proximity = Appreciation Catalyst

The Pune Ring Road at 1.8 km from Pride World City's main gate will, upon completion, enable signal-free access to Hinjawadi (25 min), Pimpri-Chinchwad (20 min), and Pune Station (28 min) — transforming Charholi from a north-east suburb into a central hub.

BRTS & PMC Bus — Public Transit Coverage

BRTS (Bus Rapid Transit System) connectivity is available on the main Nagar Road corridor 2 km from the township. Multiple PMC bus routes serve the Charholi-Lohegaon belt daily, reducing car dependency for employed residents.

Major Hospitals — Under 15 Minutes

Jehangir Hospital (Nagar Road), Pune Airport Road hospitals, and multiple multi-specialty facilities are within 12–18 minutes. Pride World City's north-east Pune location places it equidistant from both Nagar Road and Airport Road hospital belts.

Shopping & Entertainment — 10-Minute Drive

Phoenix Marketcity (Viman Nagar), Seasons Mall, and multiple retail malls on Airport Road and Nagar Road are within 10–18 minutes. Dhanori commercial market is 7 minutes away for daily needs.

Key Buyer Questions & Answers

How far is Pride World City from Pune Airport?

Pride World City Charholi is approximately 6 km / 10 minutes from Pune International Airport (Lohegaon) via Lohegaon-Charholi Road and Nagar Road, with minimal signal delays during off-peak hours.

How far is Pride World City from Kharadi IT Park?

Pride World City is approximately 11 km / 18 minutes from Kharadi EON IT Park via the Wagholi-Nagar Road route. During off-peak hours, the commute drops to 12–15 minutes.

How far is the Pune Ring Road from Pride World City?

The Pune Ring Road (PRR) passes approximately 1.8 km from Pride World City's main gate on Charholi Road. Once operational, it will provide signal-free access to Hinjawadi, Pimpri-Chinchwad, and Pune Station.

Is Pride World City well-connected to public transport?

BRTS connectivity is available on Nagar Road approximately 2 km from the township. Multiple PMC city bus routes serve the Charholi-Lohegaon belt. Propsmart Realty can provide current route details on request.

Which areas can Pride World City residents access within 20 minutes?

Within 20 minutes: Pune Airport (10 min), Viman Nagar (14 min), Dhanori commercial (7 min), Lohegaon (8 min), Kharadi EON IT Park (18 min), DY Patil Knowledge City (7 min), and multiple hospitals and malls on Airport Road and Nagar Road.
