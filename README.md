## 🌟 Executive Summary

This digital experience portal has been engineered as a **luxury real estate masterpiece** for **Pride World City**, Pune’s most iconic 400+ acre integrated mega township developed by **Pride Group**.

Blending ultra-modern aesthetics (inspired by Apple, Aman Resorts, Sobha, and Foster + Partners) with state-of-the-art interactive web engineering, this portal delivers an immersive exploration of the township ecosystem, its 8 thematic residential clusters, civic infrastructure, and financial intelligence tools.

---

## 🏛️ Key Masterpiece Modules

### 1. Interactive 400-Acre Masterplan Township Explorer
* **Vector SVG Masterplan Blueprint:** High-fidelity interactive vector rendering of all 400 acres, including residential zones, 7-acre central lake, Club Royale, 10-acre sports arena, K-12 Cambridge/CBSE school, Pride Tech IT district, 100-bed hospital, and high-street shopping boulevard.
* **Bi-directional Synchronization:** Hovering or clicking any sector on the map illuminates the sector and synchronizes with the dynamic detail cards on the side pane.
* **Canvas Controls:** Built-in Zoom In, Zoom Out, and Reset View navigation tools.

### 2. Signature Residential Clusters & Interactive Unit Finder
* **Dynamic Enclave Filtering:** Filter seamlessly by configuration (`2 BHK`, `3 BHK`, `4 BHK & Sky Villas`) or possession status (`Ready to Move`, `New Launch / Ongoing`).
* **Featured Clusters:**
  * **Kingsbury:** Royal high-rise living with double-height entrance lobbies & sky lounges.
  * **Wellington:** Resort-style residences featuring infinity lagoon pools & private floating cabanas.
  * **Montreal:** Millennial-centric smart homes powered by home automation & acoustic glass.
  * **Miami:** Waterfront coastal living overlooking the 7-acre lake with deep glass sundecks.
  * **Notting Hill:** Aristocratic 3 & 4 BHK duplex mansions with private plunge pools.
  * **Long Island:** Iconic 24th-floor sky villas offering 40-foot continuous sky terraces.
  * **Valencia:** Spanish sunset-themed 2 & 3 BHK residences with terracotta courtyards.
  * **Boston Central & SoHo:** Cosmopolitan live-work studios and high-yield urban pads.
* **Architectural Floor Plans Modal:** Interactive vector blueprints and authentic layout sheets (`2-BHK-Montreal.jpg`, `3BHK-montreal.jpg`, `3-BHK-L.png`, `3-BHK-XL.png`) displaying room dimensions, sun orientation compass, and cost sheet downloads.

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

You can open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or launch a local server:

```bash
# Using Python
python3 -m http.server 3000

# Or using Node
npx serve .
```

Then visit [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your web browser.
