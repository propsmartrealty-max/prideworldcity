Amenity & Clubhouse Questions

Does every Pride World City residential cluster get its own clubhouse and swimming pool?

Yes—in addition to central township sports zones, individual clusters like Boston Central (dual clubhouses), Wellington (resort podium clubhouse), Miami (50m pool deck), Montreal, and Valencia house exclusive cluster clubhouses, gyms, and pools.

What outdoor sports courts and fitness infrastructure exist inside the 400-acre township?

Residents access a dedicated cricket ground, multi-purpose futsal & basketball zones, badminton & pickleball courts, cycling tracks, lakeside boardwalks, open-air calisthenics decks, and shaded kids play parks.

Are any clubhouses already operational for resident families at Pride World City?

Yes—operational resident clusters such as Kingsbury, Brooklyn, Manhattan, and Wellington feature completed clubhouses, landscaped parks, and banquet facilities serving existing families on site today.
