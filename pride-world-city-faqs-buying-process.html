Why Pride World City Leads This Segment

Agreement for Sale (AFS) — MahaRERA Mandatory Protection

Under MahaRERA, all buyers must receive a registered Agreement for Sale (AFS) within 30 days of booking. The AFS legally binds the builder to deliver the specified unit by the agreed date with specific penalties for delay.

Home Loan Disbursement — Construction-Linked Plan Explained

Pride World City offers Construction-Linked Payment (CLP) plans. Banks disburse home loan amounts in tranches as construction milestones are certified by an independent engineer. This protects buyers from paying full amounts before physical completion.

Stamp Duty & Registration — What to Budget

Pune stamp duty is 6% (male buyer) or 5% (female buyer/joint). Registration fee is 1% capped at ₹30,000. Total registration cost on a ₹85L flat: approximately ₹5.4L (male) or ₹4.3L (female) + ₹30,000 registration.

MahaRERA Buyer Rights — What You're Entitled To

Every MahaRERA-registered project buyer is entitled to: quarterly construction updates, escrow account protection (70% of buyer funds in designated account), penalty for delivery delays (SBI MCLR + 2% interest), and defect liability for 5 years post-possession.

Possession Checklist — Before You Collect Keys

Before taking possession, insist on: Occupation Certificate (OC) from PMC/PCMC, all MahaRERA completion certificates, society formation or AOA registration, NOC from builder, 5-year maintenance deposit details, and detailed flat measurement verification.

Tax Benefits — 80C, 24B & Section 80EEA Explained

Home loan buyers get: Section 80C deduction for principal repayment (up to ₹1.5L/year), Section 24(b) interest deduction (up to ₹2L/year for self-occupied), and first-time buyers get additional ₹1.5L under Section 80EEA — total ₹5L annual tax benefit.

Key Buyer Questions & Answers

What is the booking amount for Pride World City flats?

The booking amount for Pride World City starts from approximately ₹2 Lacs (refundable within 7 days if you change your decision). The exact booking amount varies by cluster and configuration. Contact Propsmart Realty for current booking conditions.

What home loans are available for Pride World City Charholi?

All major banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, PNB Housing) have approved Pride World City projects for home loans. Loan-to-Value (LTV) is up to 80% of the Agreement for Sale value. NRI buyers can avail NRE/NRO-based home loans.

What is the stamp duty for Pride World City flat in Pune?

Pune stamp duty is 6% for male buyers and 5% for female or joint (female first) buyers, calculated on the Agreement for Sale value. Registration fee is 1% capped at ₹30,000. On a ₹85L flat, total stamp duty + registration is approximately ₹5.4–₹5.7 Lacs.

What is the construction-linked payment plan at Pride World City?

Pride World City's Construction-Linked Plan (CLP) ties payment milestones to construction stages verified by an independent engineer: typically 10% at booking, 10% at AFS, then 10–15% tranches at each floor slab completion. Banks disburse loan in matching tranches.

What are my rights as a MahaRERA buyer at Pride World City?

As a MahaRERA-registered project buyer, you are entitled to: quarterly construction progress updates, escrow account protection for 70% of funds, penalty interest if possession is delayed beyond agreed date, 5-year structural defect liability, and right to form society within 3 months of possession.

What documents should I check before buying at Pride World City?

Essential documents to verify: MahaRERA registration certificate (with registration number), approved building plan, land title/ownership documents, latest construction progress report from MahaRERA portal, and Agreement for Sale draft reviewed by an independent lawyer.
