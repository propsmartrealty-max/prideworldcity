Why Pride World City Leads This Segment

280 Acres of Curated Green Space — Unprecedented in Pune East

While comparable east Pune projects preserve 10–25% as open space, Pride World City's urban planning reserves 280 acres — including parks, forest trails, lake promenade, sports lawns, and biodiversity zones — exclusively for residents.

Butterfly Park — 30+ Native Species Habitat

A dedicated butterfly sanctuary with native host plants supports 30+ butterfly species. Residents can observe Common Mormon, Lime Butterfly, and Blue Tiger varieties year-round within walking distance of their homes.

Medicinal Herb Garden — Ayurvedic Wellness Corridor

A curated medicinal herb trail featuring Tulsi, Ashwagandha, Brahmi, Neem, and other Ayurvedic plants provides both educational and wellness value. This unique feature is documented in township design but rarely seen at this scale in Pune.

7-Acre Natural Lake — Ecological + Aesthetic Anchor

The 7-acre lake is not merely decorative — it serves as a rainwater harvesting reservoir, natural aquifer recharge zone, and wildlife habitat hosting migratory birds seasonally. The lake promenade is Pride World City's most photographed public space.

3.5 km Forest Trail — Circadian Health Infrastructure

Research links morning outdoor walks in green corridors to improved mental health, reduced cortisol, and better sleep. Pride World City's 3.5 km forest trail circuit, shaded and landscaped, provides this health infrastructure 365 days a year.

Sports Grounds — Cricket, Football & Multi-Sport Lawn

Open sports grounds covering multiple acres provide space for cricket matches, football practice, athletics, and township league sports — unavailable in standalone apartment projects.

Key Buyer Questions & Answers

What is the green cover area at Pride World City Charholi?

Pride World City has 70% open green cover — approximately 280 acres out of the total 400-acre township. This includes parks, forest trails, the 7-acre lake, butterfly park, herb garden, and sports grounds.

Does Pride World City have a biodiversity park?

Yes. Pride World City has a dedicated butterfly sanctuary supporting 30+ native butterfly species, a medicinal herb garden with Ayurvedic plants, and a natural lake that serves as a migratory bird habitat — forming a comprehensive biodiversity corridor.

How long is the walking trail at Pride World City?

The main forest walking trail at Pride World City is approximately 3.5 km in a circular circuit, shaded by mature trees and landscaped with native plants. Additional lake promenade and park paths add further walking options for residents.

What birds can be seen at the 7-acre lake at Pride World City?

The 7-acre natural lake attracts migratory and local bird species including Purple Moorhen, Common Kingfisher, White-breasted Waterhen, Egrets, and Cormorants during winter months. It is a recognized birding spot among township residents.

Is Pride World City the greenest township in Pune?

With 70% open green cover across 400 acres, Pride World City is among the highest green-ratio townships in Pune's eastern corridor. This is significantly higher than Pune's average residential project green ratio of 20–30%.
