Key Buyer Answers

Which clusters at Pride World City already have OC and residents living inside?

Brooklyn, Manhattan, Long Island (completed wings), Notting Hill, and completed towers in Kingsbury, SoHo, and early Wellington/Montreal wings are delivered and occupied with 5M+ sq. ft. handed over across Pride World City.

How can buyers check exact quarterly construction progress for any tower?

Enter the cluster's MahaRERA registration number (such as Valencia P52100052309 or Boston Central P52100078307) on maharera.mahaonline.gov.in to inspect Form 1 Architect Certificates, Form 2 Engineer Certificates, and statutory proposed completion dates.

Should a buyer choose Ready OC inventory or Under-Construction Valencia/Miami?

Buyers needing immediate self-use or savings from OC-exempt GST* rules typically shortlist Kingsbury/Brooklyn/Manhattan, while buyers preferring flexible construction-linked payment plans and newer tower amenities choose Valencia, Boston Central, or Miami.
