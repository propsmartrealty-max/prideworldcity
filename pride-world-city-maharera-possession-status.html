Key Buyer Answers

How can I check construction progress on MahaRERA for my tower?

Copy your target cluster MahaRERA ID (for example P52100052309 for Valencia) and enter it under Registered Projects at maharera.mahaonline.gov.in to view quarterly engineer certificates and approved plans.

Which Pride World City clusters have Zero GST due to ready possession?

Completed/OC-received phases such as Kingsbury, Brooklyn, Manhattan, and Long Island ready resale/occupancy units attract 0% GST, whereas under-construction phases carry statutory 5% GST.
