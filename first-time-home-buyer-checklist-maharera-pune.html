Buyer Legal & Tax FAQs

What taxes apply when buying a new flat in Pride World City Charholi Pune?

Under-construction MahaRERA units generally attract 5% GST alongside Maharashtra Stamp Duty (typically 7% standard in PMRDA/PCMC limits or 6% for female co-owners, subject to prevailing rules) plus ₹30,000 registration fees; ready OC-received flats carry zero GST.

How can a first-time buyer verify Pride World City MahaRERA status online?

Visit maharera.mahaonline.gov.in and enter cluster registration numbers such as P52100052309 (Valencia), P52100078307 (Boston), or PR1260002600188 (Miami) to inspect sanctioned floor plans, land title, and quarterly construction updates.

What should an All-Inclusive Cost Sheet show before paying a booking token?

Insist on a written breakdown separating Agreement Value (Basic + Floor Rise + PLC + Parking/Clubhouse), GST, Stamp Duty & Registration, and society maintenance deposit so zero surprise charges appear at registration.
