Key Buyer Answers

Is an East-facing or West-facing flat better in Pune weather?

Both offer distinct benefits in Pune's Deccan plateau climate: East-facing balconies receive gentle morning sunlight without harsh afternoon heat, whereas West-facing residences capture strong prevailing south-westerly breezes during Pune's evening and monsoon months.

Which Pride World City cluster specializes in East-West facing apartments?

Pride Valencia (MahaRERA P52100052309) is planned across 5.9 acres with East-West facing sun-side 2 & 3 BHK apartments (755–1,180 sq. ft. carpet), maximizing morning light and natural wind cross-ventilation.

What Vastu basics should homebuyers check on a Pune apartment floor plan?

Common buyer checks include an East or North main entrance door, South-East kitchen cooking zone, South-West master bedroom, open cross-ventilated living area, and avoiding dark internal passages without natural light.
