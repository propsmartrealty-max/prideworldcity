GST on Property — When It Applies and When It Doesn't

GST is 5% on under-construction flats (no input tax credit for buyer). Ready-to-move flats with Occupation Certificate (OC) attract zero GST. This GST difference can be ₹4–₹8 Lacs on a ₹85 Lacs flat — a critical cost factor.

MahaRERA — How to Verify a Builder Before Buying

Every Pune buyer should verify the builder's MahaRERA registration, project status, and pending complaints at maharera.mahaonline.gov.in before signing any document. All 9 Pride World City clusters are individually registered and complaint-free.

NRI Investment in Pune — FEMA, TDS & NRE Account Rules

NRIs can purchase residential property in Pune under FEMA 1999 without RBI approval. Payment must be from NRE/NRO account in INR. The buyer's resident Indian representative can manage transactions. TDS is 20–30% on resale by NRI.

Key Buyer Questions & Answers

What is GST on flat purchase in Pune in 2026?

GST on under-construction flats in Pune is 5% of the Agreement for Sale value with no input tax credit available to buyers. Ready-to-move flats with Occupation Certificate (OC) attract zero GST. This can be a ₹4–₹8 Lacs saving on a ₹85 Lacs flat.

How to verify a builder and project in Pune before buying?

Visit maharera.mahaonline.gov.in and search by project name or builder name. Verify: registration status (Active/Revoked), project completion date, quarterly updates submitted, and complaints filed. All 9 Pride World City clusters are MahaRERA registered and verifiable.

Can NRI buy property in Pune in 2026?

Yes. NRIs (Non-Resident Indians) can purchase residential property in Pune under FEMA 1999 without Reserve Bank of India approval. Payment must be made in INR through NRE, NRO, or FCNR bank accounts. No agricultural or plantation land is permissible.

What is the best area to buy flat in Pune in 2026 for investment?

What is the stamp duty for property in Pune in 2026?

Pune stamp duty 2026: 6% for male buyer, 5% for female or joint (female first) buyer, 4% for property in rural/gram panchayat zone. Registration fee is 1% capped at ₹30,000. Total transaction cost on ₹85L flat: approximately ₹5.4–₹5.9 Lacs.
