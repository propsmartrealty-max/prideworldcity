Frequently Asked Questions — Charholi & North Pune Real Estate

Why is Charholi Budruk the fastest-growing real estate market in North Pune?

Charholi Budruk combines 10-minute proximity to Pune Airport, 18-minute connectivity to Kharadi EON IT Park, direct access to the 128-km PMRDA Ring Road, and large-scale master-planned infrastructure like Pride World City.

What is the rental yield for flats near Pune Airport and Charholi?

1 BHK and 2 BHK apartments inside Pride World City achieve rental yields of 4.8% to 6.0% annually, outperforming Pune's 3.2% average due to constant demand from airport crew, Kharadi IT staff, and D.Y. Patil Knowledge City.

Are commercial office spaces and retail shops available in Charholi?

Yes, Pride World City features Pride Tech Park for Grade-A IT corporate offices and SoHo / Town Plaza commercial spaces for high-street retail shops.
