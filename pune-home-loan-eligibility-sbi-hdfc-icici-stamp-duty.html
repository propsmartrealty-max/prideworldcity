5 Documents to Keep Ready Before Applying in Pune

Resident Salaried & Self-Employed

PAN & Aadhaar KYC, latest 3 months salary slips / 3-year audited ITR + computation, 6-month salary bank statements, Form 16, and the developer allotment letter + MahaRERA certificate copy.

NRI Buyers (UAE, USA, UK, Singapore)

Valid passport & visa/OCI, overseas employment contract & salary credits into NRE/NRO account, overseas credit bureau report, and attested Power of Attorney (PoA) if registering via a family representative in Pune.

Key Buyer Answers

How much home loan can a Pune buyer get on a ₹75L to ₹1 Cr flat?

Subject to credit score and income FOIR norms, RBI Loan-to-Value guidelines generally allow banks to finance up to 75%–80% of property cost above ₹75 Lakhs and up to 80% between ₹30L–₹75L, with actual sanction depending on individual bank underwriting.

Which major banks have APF approvals for Pride World City clusters?

Leading public and private sector banks including State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, Axis Bank, Bank of Baroda, and Punjab National Bank finance eligible MahaRERA-registered clusters in Pride World City subject to borrower eligibility.

How much does female ownership save on Pune stamp duty?

Under Maharashtra Government policy, exclusively female property buyers receive a 1% stamp duty rebate in Pune (typically 6% instead of 7% for male or mixed-gender joint registration), saving ₹90,000 on a ₹90 Lakh Agreement Value, subject to prevailing statutory lock-in rules.
