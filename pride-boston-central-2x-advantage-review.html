Why Pride World City Leads This Segment

2X Advantage — Dual Clubhouse Concept Explained

Boston Central's signature 2X Advantage means every resident has access to 2 fully-equipped clubhouses without walking across the township. This eliminates the 'distant clubhouse' problem common in large townships and ensures amenity access at doorstep.

Dual Balcony 2 BHK — Maximum Ventilation & Light

Every Boston 2 BHK unit has 2 balconies — one in the living area and one in the master bedroom. This dual-balcony design maximizes natural ventilation, reduces air conditioning dependency, and creates a cross-breeze that improves indoor air quality significantly.
