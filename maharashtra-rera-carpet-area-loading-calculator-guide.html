MahaRERA Formula Definition: Under the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act 2016, RERA Carpet Area equals the net usable floor area inside apartment walls (including internal partition walls) excluding external walls, exclusive balconies, service verandahs, and open terraces. Legally, pricing must be evaluated against statutory RERA Carpet + itemized Balcony area.

Key Buyer Answers

What is included in MahaRERA carpet area vs exclusive balcony in Maharashtra?

Under MahaRERA, RERA Carpet Area covers the net usable floor area inside apartment walls (including internal partition walls) and excludes external walls, service shafts, and exclusive balconies or open terraces, which are itemized separately on the sanctioned plan.

What is a reasonable loading percentage for apartments in Pune?

In Pune high-rise townships with double lobbies and multiple lifts, total loading between RERA carpet and saleable area typically ranges around 28%–35%. Buyers should compare price strictly on Total Usable Area (RERA Carpet + Balcony) rather than marketing super built-up claims.

How do I verify sanctioned carpet area for any Pride World City tower?

Match the unit typology against the cluster MahaRERA ID (such as Valencia P52100052309 or Boston P52100078307) on maharera.mahaonline.gov.in under Sanctioned Building Plans and Floor Plans before paying booking confirmation.
