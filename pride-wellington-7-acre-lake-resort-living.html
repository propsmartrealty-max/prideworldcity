Why Pride World City Leads This Segment

7-Acre Lake Frontage — Pune's Only Lake-Front Township Cluster

Wellington's podium is designed to maximize lake views from multiple aspects. Lake-facing units command premium positioning within the township, with morning mist, bird activity, and evening reflections creating a resort hotel ambience within a residential tower.

Podium-Level Design — Elevated Amenity Deck

Wellington's podium deck — elevated above road level — houses a resort-style pool, cabanas, landscaped gardens, and seating areas overlooking the lake. This design creates a private amenity deck inaccessible to non-residents.

3 BHK & 4 BHK Configurations — Family-Scale Living

Wellington caters to upgrade buyers and families seeking spacious 3 BHK and 4 BHK configurations. The larger carpet areas (900–1,500+ sq.ft) accommodate multi-generational families, home offices, and lifestyle requirements beyond entry-level housing.

MahaRERA P52100054534 — Verified & Active

Wellington's MahaRERA registration (P52100054534) is current, verifiable, and covers all tower configurations. RERA-mandated quarterly updates provide buyers with construction progress transparency.

Biodiversity-Rich Lake Ecosystem — Natural Living Premium

The 7-acre lake alongside Wellington hosts Purple Moorhen, Kingfisher, Egret, and seasonal migratory birds. Residents report a unique 'nature sounds at dawn' experience that urban apartments cannot simulate.

Lake Premium — Historical 15–20% Above Non-Lake Units

Data from comparable lake-front township projects in Pune shows lake-facing units command 15–20% premium at resale over similar non-lake configurations. Wellington buyers benefit from this structural appreciation advantage.
