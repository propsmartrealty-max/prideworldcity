Key Buyer Answers

Does Pride World City include dedicated pet parks inside the township?

Yes, Pride World City's masterplan and clusters such as Pride Valencia incorporate fenced Bark Park / Pet Park agility zones separated from children's play areas, along with wide green walking boulevards.

Which home configurations suit families with dogs or large pets?

Spacious 2 & 3 BHK residences with deep balconies in Valencia and Boston Central (dual balconies), or ground/podium-accessible 3 & 4 BHK units in Wellington, Montreal, and Notting Hill work exceptionally well for pet owners.

Why is an integrated 400-acre township safer for daily dog walks than city streets?

Wide internal security-monitored footpaths, separated vehicular lanes, and shaded park belts protect pets and owners from stray pack conflicts and fast city traffic.
