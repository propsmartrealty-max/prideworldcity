Understanding Your Cost Sheet, Stamp Duty & Payment Milestones

  • Agreement Value vs All-Inclusive Cost Sheet: Base agreement price excludes statutory stamp duty (7% in Pune municipal limits; 1% rebate for sole/joint female ownership under Maharashtra norms), registration fees (₹30,000 flat cap above threshold), and applicable GST (5% non-affordable under-construction; 0% GST on Occupancy Certificate received ready units in Kingsbury/Brooklyn).
  • Booking Token & CLP Schedule: Expression of Interest (EOI) / allotment tokens typically range from ₹1 Lac to ₹2 Lacs refundable prior to allotment, followed by 10% booking completion upon Agreement for Sale execution, and MahaRERA-regulated Construction-Linked Payment (CLP) tranches tied strictly to plinth, slab casting, brickwork, and possession milestones.
  • Pre-Approved Bank Loans: State Bank of India (SBI), HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank provide up to 80-90% Loan-to-Value (LTV) based on income eligibility. Try the interactive Dynamic EMI Intelligence Simulator to test scenarios.
