Frequently Asked Questions — Pride Tech Park & Commercial

Does Pride World City have an IT park inside the township?

Yes, Pride Tech Park is the designated commercial and IT business district within the 400-acre Pride World City masterplan.

Are retail shops available for investment inside Pride World City?

Yes, high-street commercial shops and F&B spaces are integrated along the Town Plaza and cluster retail frontages serving the township population.

How do commercial office rents in Charholi compare with Viman Nagar and Kharadi?

Commercial spaces in Pride Tech Park and SoHo offer significantly lower acquisition costs per sq.ft than Viman Nagar/Kharadi while commanding rising B2B lease demand due to Pune Airport proximity.
