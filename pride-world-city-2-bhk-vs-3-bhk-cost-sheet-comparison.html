Key Buyer Answers

How much extra does a 3 BHK cost over a 2 BHK at Pride World City?

On base agreement price, moving from a flagship 2 BHK (Boston ~₹68L* or Valencia ~₹92.49L*) to a 3 BHK (Wellington ~₹1.12Cr* or Valencia ~₹1.23Cr*) typically adds ~₹25–₹38 Lakhs, translating to roughly ₹19,000–₹27,000 additional indicative monthly EMI across a 20-year tenure.

When does buying a 3 BHK upfront beat upgrading from a 2 BHK after 5 years?

Buying a 3 BHK upfront avoids repeating Maharashtra 6%–7% stamp duty, brokerage, and interior shifting costs 5 years later, which often outweigh the marginal EMI gap for families with growing children or elderly parents.

Which typologies offer higher rental yield percentages vs long-term family retention?

1 & 2 BHK apartments generally command higher percentage gross rental yield (~4.8%–6%) due to lower purchase cost, while 3 BHK homes deliver stronger end-user family permanence and lower tenant turnover.
