Corridor Buyer & Investor Questions

How does Charholi compare with Kharadi and Baner for 3 BHK buyers in 2026?

While prime Baner and Upper Kharadi 3 BHK residences frequently exceed ₹1.55 Cr to ₹2.10 Cr+, Pride World City in Charholi delivers comparable carpet dimensions inside a 400-acre gated township starting between ₹98 Lakh* and ₹1.35 Cr*.

Why are long-term investors rotating from saturated Hinjawadi into North-East Pune Charholi?

Hinjawadi faces acute single-artery bottleneck congestion, whereas Charholi benefits from multiple wide-road links toward Pune International Airport, Viman Nagar, Kharadi, Chakan/Bhosari auto hubs, and the upcoming Pune Ring Road.

Does Charholi have true integrated township inventory like Magarpatta or Amanora?

Yes—Pride World City spans 400 continuous acres in Charholi Budruk with operational schools, multi-acre landscaped parks, 10 residential clusters, Town Plaza retail, and upcoming Pride Tech Park.
