Pride World City — 36 FAQs & Buyer Answers

Transparent, verified answers across every residential cluster, pricing sheet, MahaRERA registration, Pune Airport & Kharadi distance, resale market, and rental yield benchmark.

Township Overview

What is Pride World City Pune?

Pride World City is a landmark 400-acre integrated mega township developed by Pride Group in Charholi Budruk, North Pune. Comprising 5+ million sq. ft. of completed construction and 8+ million sq. ft. under active development across 12 residential and commercial enclaves, it integrates homes, Pride Tech Park, GIIS Cambridge School, and a 7-acre freshwater lake.

Township Overview

Where is Pride World City located?

Pride World City is located at Charholi Budruk, adjacent to D.Y. Patil Knowledge City, Pune - 412105, strategically positioned between Lohegaon, Dhanori, Viman Nagar, and the Kharadi IT corridor.

Township Overview

Who is the developer of Pride World City?

The master developer is Pride Group (Pride Developers Pune), led by Managing Director Mr. Arvind Jain, carrying a 28+ year track record of delivering residential townships, commercial IT hubs, and luxury hospitality projects across Pune, Mumbai, and Bangalore.

Township Overview

How many acres is Pride World City township and how much open space does it have?

The township spans approximately 400 contiguous acres with 70% dedicated open greens, botanical trails, a 7-acre central freshwater lake promenade, and a 10-acre multi-sport arena.

Township Overview

How many families currently live inside Pride World City?

Over 10,000 families form the envisioned and thriving multi-generational township ecosystem across delivered clusters such as Kingsbury, Brooklyn, Manhattan, Long Island, and Notting Hill.

Township Overview

Is Pride World City a self-sustaining city within a city?

Yes. Built around the 15-minute city blueprint, Pride World City integrates residential towers, Pride Tech Park IT offices, Global Indian International School (K-12), Town Plaza shopping, planned 100-bed hospital, senior citizen plazas, and on-site BRTS transit.

There are 12 distinct internal enclaves: Valencia (New Launch sun-side 2 & 3 BHK), Miami (waterfront 2, 3 & 4.5 BHK), Boston Central (2X advantage 2 BHK), Montreal (smart tech 2, 3 BHK & 4 BHK duplex), Wellington (7-acre podium resort 2 & 3 BHK), Kingsbury (ready royal 2 & 3 BHK), Brooklyn (urban 1 & 2 BHK), Manhattan (cosmopolitan 2 & 3 BHK), Atlantic (scenic 2 & 3 BHK), Notting Hill (manor duplexes w/ plunge pools), Long Island (4 BHK sky villas), and SoHo (live-work suites).

What payment plan options and home loans are available?

Pricing & Finance

What is the difference between agreement value and all-inclusive cost sheet?

Agreement value covers the base property cost, whereas an all-inclusive cost sheet incorporates floor rise, PLC, club charges, legal documentation, GST (for under-construction units; 0% GST on OC-received ready units), stamp duty (typically 7% in Pune district / 6% for female co-owners), and registration.

Yes. Charholi property prices appreciated ~42.8% over 5 years driven by airport expansion and Ring Road construction, yet remain 30-45% lower per sq.ft than mature neighboring markets like Viman Nagar (₹11,500-₹16,500/sq.ft) and Kharadi (₹8,900-₹13,200/sq.ft).
