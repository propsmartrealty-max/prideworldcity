Why Pride World City Leads This Segment

Road Infrastructure — Four-Lane Widening in Progress

The Charholi Budruk main road (connecting to Nagar Road and Lohegaon) is undergoing four-lane widening as part of the BRTS corridor development. Expected completion aligns with the Pune Ring Road timeline, dramatically improving connectivity.

Electricity Supply — Dedicated MSEDCL Feeder

Pride World City has a dedicated MSEDCL electrical feeder, reducing outage exposure significantly compared to village-grid properties in Charholi. DG backup covers critical common area infrastructure during load-shedding hours.

Hospitals — Airport Road Belt Within 12 Minutes

Multiple multi-specialty hospitals on Airport Road (Lohegaon belt) including Jehangir, Deenanath Mangeshkar (Airport Road branch), and Columbia Asia are within 12–15 minutes via Charholi-Lohegaon Road.

Schools & Education — GIIS Flagship + DY Patil University

GIIS K-12 within Pride World City and DY Patil Knowledge City campus 7 minutes away on Charholi Road make this location exceptional for families. CBSE, ICSE, and international curriculum options exist within the community.

Water Supply Reliability — PMC + Township Lake System

Charholi village faces seasonal PMC supply gaps. However, Pride World City's self-contained 7-acre lake + STP water system is completely independent of PMC supply, making it immune to municipal water cuts that affect standalone Charholi projects.

Retail & Daily Needs — Dhanori Market + Township Plaza

Dhanori commercial market (7 min) covers daily groceries, vegetables, and services. The township's Town Plaza includes curated retail and food & beverage within Pride World City's boundary, reducing external dependency for daily needs.

Key Buyer Questions & Answers

Is water supply reliable in Charholi Budruk?

For standalone Charholi village projects, PMC supply is seasonal with gaps in summer. Inside Pride World City, the 7-acre lake + dual STP system makes water supply completely independent of PMC, ensuring 24×7 supply year-round without tankers.

What are the roads like in Charholi Pune?

Charholi main road is undergoing four-lane widening. Internal township roads at Pride World City are 24–36 meter master-planned boulevards. Village lanes in standalone projects are narrower. Overall connectivity is improving significantly with ring road and BRTS development.

Which hospitals are near Charholi Budruk?

Multiple hospitals are accessible within 12–18 minutes: Jehangir Hospital (Nagar Road, 15 min), Columbia Asia (Airport Road, 12 min), and Deenanath Mangeshkar (Airport Road branch, 15 min). Emergency response times are acceptable for the zone.

What schools are available near Pride World City Charholi?

GIIS K-12 (K-12 International) is within Pride World City campus. DY Patil International School and Knowledge City campus are 7 minutes on Charholi Road. Additionally, multiple CBSE and state board schools operate in Dhanori (7 min) and Lohegaon (10 min).

Is Charholi Budruk safe to live in?

Pride World City Charholi has 24×7 gated security, boom barriers, CCTV across all internal roads, trained security personnel, and visitor management systems. The township's 10,000+ resident community also creates natural neighbourhood security through density.

What is the electricity supply situation in Charholi?

Pride World City has a dedicated MSEDCL electrical feeder with DG backup for common area infrastructure. Township residents report significantly fewer outages than standalone Charholi village properties. Solar infrastructure across common areas further reduces grid dependency.
