Key Buyer Answers

Should families buy Pride Valencia or Pride Miami in 2026?

Choose Valencia (from ₹92.49L*) for dedicated East-West sun orientation, 5.9-acre thematic green trails, and newer G+28 tower planning; pick Miami (from ₹92L*) for Art-Deco lakeside proximity and larger 4.5 BHK options up to 2,240 sq. ft.

How does Pride Wellington differ from Miami and Valencia?

Pride Wellington centers on an expansive 7-acre landscaped vehicle-free resort podium overlooking the central lake with floating cabanas, acoustic work pods, and 3 BHK layouts up to 1,420 sq. ft.
