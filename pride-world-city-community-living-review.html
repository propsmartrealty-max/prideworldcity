Why Pride World City Leads This Segment

10,000+ Families — Self-Sustaining Urban Community

Pride World City is home to 10,000+ families across Brooklyn, SoHo, Long Island, Boston, Montreal, Wellington, Kingsbury, Miami, and Valencia clusters. This density creates a self-sustaining social ecosystem with organic communities, sports leagues, resident welfare associations, and social events.

GIIS K-12 Campus — International Education at Your Doorstep

Global Indian International School (GIIS) K-12 campus operates from within the township boundary. Children walk or cycle to school without leaving the gated perimeter — a rarity in Indian real estate and a lifestyle premium most buyers didn't know was possible.

7-Acre Natural Lake — The Social & Ecological Heartbeat

The 7-acre natural lake forms the visual and ecological anchor of Pride World City. Morning walks along the promenade, evening pavilion gatherings, and birdwatching from the shore create a resort lifestyle that daily suburban living rarely offers.

50,000 Sq.Ft Club Royale — 5-Star Recreation

Club Royale houses Olympic-length pool, temperature-controlled gymnasium, squash courts, billiards, indoor games hall, kids' zone, rooftop terrace lounge, and multiple sports courts. It is one of the largest township clubhouses in Pune's east corridor.

70% Green Cover — Biodiversity Park & Forest Trails

280 acres of the 400-acre township is maintained as open green space: butterfly gardens, medicinal herb trails, forest walking paths, and manicured lawns. No other Pune east project offers this green ratio.

24×7 Gated Security — Safe for Families & Seniors

Boom barriers, CCTV surveillance across all arterial roads, trained security personnel, visitor management systems, and intercom connectivity at every unit ensure complete peace of mind for residents including senior citizens and families with children.
